# About WonderWhere

Plan the day. Keep the destination a surprise.

WonderWhere began as a way to guide someone through a proposal day, one place at a time. The proposal went well. This app keeps that memory alive and lets you make an adventure of your own.

Build a route around places that matter to you, add a hint or something to do at each stop, and let the compass lead the way. The next destination stays a surprise until you arrive.
