# WonderWhere permission and system text

Applied to the app's configuration and user-visible strings. These explanations describe the requested feature; the iOS permission dialog supplies its own buttons and surrounding text.

## Location — While Using the App

Your location guides you to saved places and records arrivals during an active trip, including while your phone is locked.

## Calendar — Full Access

WonderWhere uses calendar access to list your calendars, add trip events to the calendar you choose, and find its own events when you ask to update them.

## Alarms

WonderWhere schedules the trip-start, departure, and deadline alarms you choose.

## Notification title

WonderWhere

## Before the trip starts

Your trip is starting soon. Open WonderWhere for the planned start time.

## Before leaving a place

It's almost time to leave this place. Open WonderWhere for your planned departure.

## Before an appointment or deadline

A trip appointment or deadline is coming up. Open WonderWhere for details.

## Arrival notification

Open WonderWhere to see this place.

## Alarm title

WonderWhere alert

## Location denied

Allow location access for WonderWhere in Settings.

The location background mode is retained for active-trip tracking. No Always-location prompt, contacts prompt, tracking-consent prompt, or remote-push service has been added. Notifications and calendars remain optional. Existing notification identifiers and calendar ownership references remain stable across the rename.
