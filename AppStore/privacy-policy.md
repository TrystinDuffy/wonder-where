# WonderWhere Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2, 2026

WonderWhere is an iPhone app developed by Trystin Duffy for planning trips and discovering saved places with a compass. This policy explains the information the app uses, the services involved, and your choices.

## Your plans and trip history

WonderWhere stores your reusable plans, saved places, coordinates and areas, hints, instructions, schedules, trip progress, visit history, notification preferences, and appearance settings on your device. If you use calendar export, the app also stores references to the calendars and events it creates or updates.

There is no WonderWhere account or developer-operated server that receives your trip library. The app does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs, and Trystin Duffy does not sell your personal information or use it for advertising tracking.

Your device's backup settings may include WonderWhere's data in an iCloud or computer backup. These backups are separate from the app's local storage and are managed through Apple and your device settings.

## Location and compass

With your permission, WonderWhere uses your location, reported location accuracy, and compass heading to point toward saved places, estimate travel, and recognize arrival and departure. During an active trip, location updates can continue while the screen is locked or the app is in the background. Active-trip tracking stops when the trip is completed or canceled. Location may also be used while choosing a place or preparing a trip.

Trip history records visit milestones and saved place details. It is not a continuous recording of every location sample. The app also stores starting coordinates and coordinates associated with saved travel estimates.

You can change location access and Precise Location in iPhone Settings. Restricting access can limit guidance, arrival detection, and travel estimates.

## Apple Maps and other external services

WonderWhere uses Apple's MapKit services for maps, place search, and travel estimates. These features send relevant information to Apple, such as search terms, the map area being requested, route start and destination coordinates, transport mode, and expected departure time. A route's starting coordinates may be your current location. Tapping a place's map opens that place in Apple Maps.

Apple processes information under its own policies. See [Apple Maps & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/apple-maps/) and [Location Services & Privacy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/location-services/). Map search, map imagery, and updated travel estimates may require an internet connection.

## Calendar access and sharing

Calendar access is optional. If you choose to add or update a trip in Calendar, WonderWhere requests full calendar access so it can show your available calendars, find previously exported events, and save the changes you request. The app uses calendar and event information on your device for this feature. It does not upload your calendar library to a developer-operated server.

Exported events can contain the trip and place names, addresses, coordinates, planned or recorded times, deadlines, hints, and arrival instructions. Calendars connected to iCloud, Google, or another account may sync these events through that provider. The Google Calendar option uses a calendar account already configured on your iPhone; WonderWhere does not receive your Google password. Those providers' privacy policies apply, including [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

You can also export a calendar file through the iOS share sheet. The service or recipient you choose receives that file and its contents. Shared files and calendar events can reveal destinations before arrival. Calendar sharing and public links are controlled by the calendar provider, and recipients may retain copies.

You can revoke calendar access in iPhone Settings. Removing access or deleting WonderWhere does not remove events or files already exported; manage those copies in Calendar, the receiving app, or the relevant account.

## Notifications and alarms

Notifications and alarms are optional and require the relevant iOS permission. WonderWhere schedules them through Apple's on-device notification and alarm systems and stores their settings and scheduling status locally. Notifications may appear on the lock screen according to your settings. You can disable them in the app or change their permissions in iPhone Settings.

## Your PIN and data controls

The optional configuration PIN prevents accidental destination spoilers. It is stored locally as an app preference and can be reset in the app. It is not encryption or a substitute for your iPhone's passcode.

You can edit places and delete reusable plans. Deleting a reusable plan keeps trips already created from it. Saved trip history remains in the app; this version does not provide a separate trip-history deletion or erase-all control.

To remove the app's current local data, use **Delete App** in iPhone Settings. **Offload App** preserves app data. Device backups, exported calendar events, shared files, and recipients' copies are managed separately and may remain after deletion.

## Support and diagnostics

If you contact Trystin Duffy for support, your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach are used to respond and resolve the request. Email is processed by the email providers involved. Support correspondence may be retained to follow up on the issue; you can request its deletion by email, subject to any applicable recordkeeping obligations.

WonderWhere has no custom analytics or crash-reporting service. Apple may process diagnostics and usage information according to your device settings and [Apple's Device Analytics & Privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/device-analytics/). If you choose to share a diagnostic report with the developer, it can be used to investigate the reported issue.

## This website

WonderWhere's public website, privacy policy, and support pages are hosted by GitHub Pages. The website does not use analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when serving these pages. See the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) for information about its hosting practices. This website hosting is separate from the app's on-device trip storage.
