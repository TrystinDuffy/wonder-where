## Capture guidance

- Suggested fictional aliases: **Coffee stop**, **Garden walk**, and **Dinner together**. Use public locations chosen for the screenshots, not the developer's existing outing. Names are aliases; screenshots must still show real app behavior with valid locations and times.
- Use short sample instructions such as **Find a spot by the flowers** and **Take a photo together**. Avoid proposal details or actual private messages from the developer's saved trip.
- Use a dedicated sample library. Exclude personal trip names, addresses, location history, configuration PINs, email addresses, and calendar account identifiers.
- Show the ordinary release interface. Simulator location injection controls must not appear in customer screenshots.
- Capture both approach and arrival as separate screenshots. Do not place a destination's name on the unreached compass screen in a way that suggests the app reveals it early.
- Keep alerts and calendar claims optional. A local calendar save does not prove Google synchronization, and no screenshot should claim automatic calendar sharing.
- Map rendering and travel estimates must have finished for the captured state. Do not replace a missing map with an invented image or suggest that map data is guaranteed offline.
- Pricing is unconfirmed. Do not add **Free**, subscription, discount, or purchase language.
- The screenshot order is a recommendation. Export at the sizes requested by App Store Connect for the supported iPhone display classes when the final assets are produced.
