# WonderWhere Help

Plan the day. Keep the destination a surprise.

WonderWhere helps you build an outing with places, hints, arrival instructions, and a schedule. During a trip, its compass points toward the next place and reveals the details when you arrive.

## Create your first outing

Open a reusable plan, or choose New plan on Home. Add a place, give it a name, and choose its location on the map. You can search for a location, move the map, or enter coordinates. Set the arrival area, then add any hint, ordered instructions, and time you want to spend there. Add more places in the order you want to visit them.

Schedule the plan to create a separate trip. Choose its start time and fill in any appointments or deadlines you added. Review the schedule, then choose Start trip when you are ready to go. Editing or deleting the original reusable plan does not erase trips already created from it.

## Understand the compass

The arrow points directly toward the current target. It does not follow roads or provide turn-by-turn directions. Use suitable routes, obey signs and access restrictions, and stop somewhere safe before interacting with your phone. You can open a revealed place in Apple Maps for more map detail.

The distance is a rough straight-line measurement shown in quarter-mile steps. GPS accuracy and the arrival-area size are shown separately. The search symbol appears when the app cannot determine a useful direction from the current location and heading readings.

## Improve location and arrival detection

Allow location access and Precise Location for WonderWhere in iPhone Settings. Move to a place with a clear view of the sky and away from strong magnetic interference if the heading seems wrong. Buildings, indoor spaces, tunnels, and magnetic accessories can affect location or heading readings.

An automatic arrival requires a location inside the Entry area whose reported accuracy is no worse than that area's radius. A very small area can therefore take longer to confirm. The organizer can adjust the area or confirm a missed arrival or Exit manually from the trip's details.

## Entry, Explore, and Exit

Entry is where arrival is recorded and the place's details are revealed. Explore is the area you want to spend time in. Exit is where the visit finishes after you choose Next. These can share one area or be configured separately.

After arrival, the place's map and instructions stay revealed. If you move away, a small arrow can guide you back toward Explore. Next requests departure; if Exit is somewhere else, guidance continues toward it before the next place begins. Back reviews previous visits. Resume returns to current navigation without undoing progress.

## Use WonderWhere offline

Saved places, hints, instructions, the compass, and recorded trip progress work without an internet connection when the phone can obtain usable location and heading readings. Visit countdowns use the times stored on your phone.

Place search, uncached map imagery, and fresh Apple Maps travel estimates need a connection. Previously saved estimates may be shown as cached; current arrival or completion estimates may become unavailable. WonderWhere does not currently download map regions or an entire plan's maps for offline use. Calendar syncing also depends on the selected account and its connection.

## Keep a trip running while the phone is locked

Start the trip while WonderWhere is open. An active trip can continue receiving location updates while you use another app or lock the phone, subject to iOS and location permissions. Completion or cancellation stops the active trip's background location tracking.

Force-quitting the app, restarting the phone, or interruption by iOS can leave gaps in observed arrivals. Reopening WonderWhere restores saved progress; it does not invent visits during a gap. Foreground arrivals produce haptic feedback. For a locked phone, you can enable the optional arrival notification.

## Understand the schedule

A reusable plan holds places and time requirements. A scheduled trip adds real dates and times. Visit durations can be fixed or flexible. The live trip uses its current allocated visit times when calculating planned departures and estimated completion.

An appointment means arrive by that time, with the visit budget beginning no earlier than the appointment. Arrive by is an arrival deadline without required waiting. Leave by is a departure deadline that still needs enough time for the configured visit. An impossible combination of visits, travel, and deadlines needs an itinerary or time change.

Travel times are estimates. Cached routes, traffic, location uncertainty, and time spent moving within a place can change the outcome. Review any shortened visits or skipped optional places before relying on an adjusted schedule.

## Notifications and alarms

Notifications are optional. In a trip's Notifications settings, choose whether to be notified before the trip starts, before leaving a place, or before an appointment or deadline. You can also enable a notification when an arrival is recorded. Allow notification access when requested.

On iOS 26 or later, supported devices can also use the alarm option after authorization. Scheduled system alerts may be delivered while the app is closed, but schedule changes need the app to run or receive location updates before alerts can be reconciled. Notification settings, Focus, and iOS behavior can affect delivery. Check the displayed scheduling status and delivery time.

## Add or share a calendar

Open a trip's sharing options to export an iCalendar (.ics) file or add events to a calendar on your iPhone. Shared files are snapshots; sending one does not keep the recipient's calendar in sync with later trip edits. Use the app's update action when you want to update a connected calendar.

For Google Calendar, first add the Google account in iPhone Settings and enable Calendars, then select the intended calendar in WonderWhere. WonderWhere uses that iOS calendar connection; it does not provide a separate Google sign-in. The Google website is used to create a new Google calendar. Your calendar provider controls syncing and calendar sharing with other people.

## Protect the surprise

Set an optional configuration PIN in Home's Settings to help prevent accidental access to organizer pages during a trip. This PIN hides spoilers; it is not encryption or a security boundary for sensitive information. Use your iPhone's passcode and privacy settings to protect the device.

## Keep or remove your data

Your plans and trip history are stored locally. Deleting a reusable plan keeps trips already made from it. The current version does not have an in-app command to erase the entire library or delete an individual trip from history.

Deleting the app removes its local app data; offloading the app keeps its documents and data. Device or computer backups may retain copies under your backup settings. Events already added to a calendar, alarms managed by iOS, and files or messages you shared are separate from the local trip library. Cancel unwanted trip alerts before removing the app and manage exported content in the service where you saved or shared it.

## Privacy

WonderWhere has no account system, advertising, or third-party tracking SDK. Its privacy policy explains local storage, Apple Maps requests, optional calendar sharing, system backups, and support email.
