## Physical acceptance remaining

Automated checks establish the behavior of the implemented engine and interface; they do not establish physical sensor accuracy or system delivery on a particular phone. These checks still require the device in use:

1. Compare a known mapped bearing with Apple Compass using true north and the same portrait phone orientation. Rotate, hold still, move, open configuration, and return to the compass.
2. Observe Entry and Exit while locked, including a long stationary visit followed by movement. Confirm progress after unlocking, then force-quit and reopen without expecting observations during the gap.
3. Tap Next and immediately lock; verify its saved phase on reopening. Repeat navigation with network access disabled.
4. Enable the chosen alarm or notification delivery, check the actual scheduled time, and verify delivery, updates, and cancellation. Foreground arrival should produce three haptic pulses; background arrival does not promise custom haptics.

Do not treat a successful build or installation as confirmation that these physical checks passed.
